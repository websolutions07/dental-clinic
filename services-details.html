Preventive & Diagnostic Care

Preserving Natural Tooth Structure for Life

Preventive dentistry is the foundation of lifelong oral wellness. Our gentle hygiene protocol eliminates harmful oral biofilms, remineralizes early micro-enamel lesions, and protects periodontal ligaments before cavities or gum disease can compromise your smile.

Our 4-Step Preventive Protocol

1

Digital Intraoral 3D Imaging & Charting

High-resolution optical scan and digital transillumination detect micro-fractures, early demineralization, and hidden interproximal decay without heavy radiation.

2

Piezo-Electric Ultrasonic Scaling

Microscopic acoustic vibrations gently lift stubborn tartar and plaque deposits without scratching sensitive enamel.

3

Guided Air-Flow Polishing & Fluoride Shield

Gentle glycine micro-spray eliminates coffee and tea stains followed by high-strength enamel remineralization.

4

Custom Home Biome Health Prescription

Tailored interdental brush sizing, pH balancing advice, and a 6-month proactive wellness tracking schedule.

Routine hygiene cleanings save you thousands in avoided treatments and preserve natural teeth for life.

Discomfort:None (Painless Care)

Oral Reconstruction & Implants

Restoring Full Chewing Power, Comfort & Strength

Missing or damaged teeth compromise your nutrition, speech, and jaw bone structure. Our restorative team utilizes hospital-grade titanium implants, bio-inert zirconia crowns, and precision composite bonding to permanently rehabilitate your oral health, delivering 100% restored bite force indistinguishable from natural teeth.

Our 4-Step Restorative Process

1

3D Cone Beam CT Volumetric Scan

High-definition 3D cone beam tomography maps bone volume, nerve channels, and adjacent tooth roots with sub-millimeter precision.

2

Computer-Guided Site Preparation

Precision surgical guides guarantee ideal implant angulation or gentle laser-assisted decay removal with maximum tooth preservation.

3

CAD/CAM Monolithic Zirconia Milling

Custom porcelain crowns and ceramic abutments engineered to match your exact natural enamel translucency and bite shape.

4

T-Scan Computerized Occlusal Bite Balancing

Digital sensors calculate biting pressure distribution across each millimeter, preventing premature wear and guaranteeing comfort.

Clear Aligners & Orthodontics

Straight Teeth & Balanced Jaw Alignment Without Metal

Misaligned teeth and improper bite relationships are more than aesthetic concerns; they cause uneven tooth wear, chronic jaw clenching, and difficulty cleaning tight contacts. With our virtually invisible clear aligners and low-profile aesthetic braces, you can straighten your teeth comfortably, discreetly, and in up to half the time of conventional braces.

Our 4-Step Alignment Journey

1

iTero 5D Optical 3D Scanning

6,000 optical frames per second capture every millimetric contour, eliminating messy, gag-inducing putty impressions.

2

Custom 3D Computer Treatment Simulation

Proprietary biomechanical algorithms map tooth movement progression week by week, displaying your final smile before starting.

3

BPA-Free Clear Aligner Tray Delivery

Medical-grade SmartTrack thermoplastic trays apply gentle, continuous forces, easily removable for meals and brushing.

4

Refinement & Lifetime Retainer Protection

Final micro-alignment polishing followed by durable nighttime retainers to safeguard your straight, healthy smile forever.
