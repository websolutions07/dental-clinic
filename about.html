Our Story

A Journey of Trusted Dental Care, Built
on Compassion and Innovation for Over Two Decades

Founded in 2004 by Dr. Olivia Thompson and Dr. Rishi, Apurva's Dental began with a revolutionary premise: dental visits should be welcoming, transparent, and completely free of anxiety. Over the past 22 years, we have grown into an internationally acclaimed center for restorative, aesthetic, and preventive dental medicine—combining human warmth with hospital-grade technology.

2004 • The Founding Vision

Gentle, Patient-First Care

Opened our flagship practice with two operatories and a steadfast promise: every patient is treated like family with zero fear and 100% painless numbing techniques.

2011 • Digital Precision

Pioneering Digital Imaging

Eliminated uncomfortable silicone impression trays by introducing 3D digital intraoral scanners, reducing appointment times by 40% and enhancing diagnostic accuracy.

2017 • Surgical Mastery

Hospital-Grade Implantology

Dr. Emman Collins joined as Surgical Director, commissioning our sterile surgical suites and introducing computer-guided All-on-4 immediate restorations.

2022 • Aesthetic Innovation

iTero 5D Optical Simulation

Equipped every consultation suite with optical AI caries detection and real-time 3D smile outcome simulators so patients can preview their smile before treatment begins.

2026 • Apurva's Dental Today

50,000+ Smiles Restored

Celebrating over 50,000 satisfied patient visits, a 4.9/5.0 Google rating, and a team of 12 multidisciplinary dental specialists leading the future of oral health.
