Cookie Policy

Information about how Apurva's Dental utilizes web cookies and local storage.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are compact text files stored on your device that enable our interactive features—such as the 3D Tooth Model simulator settings, VITA shade preferences, and online appointment booking workflows—to function smoothly.

2. Essential Functional Cookies

These technical cookies are required for website navigation, accessibility preferences, and secure form submissions. Disabling essential cookies may prevent interactive clinical tools from rendering correctly.

3. Performance & Analytics

We collect aggregated, anonymized usage telemetry to measure page speed, procedural tool engagement, and user navigation patterns to continuously refine our digital patient experience.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can adjust or block cookies at any time via your browser settings. For any privacy inquiries, reach our digital support desk at hello@apurvasdental.com.
