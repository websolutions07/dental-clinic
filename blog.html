How often should I visit the dentist? +−

We recommend a routine dental checkup and professional prophylaxis cleaning every six months to maintain optimal oral health, eliminate tartar deposits, and detect minor decay before it requires extensive treatment.

Do you offer emergency dental services? +−

Yes, we prioritize same-day emergency appointments for acute toothaches, fractured teeth, knocked-out teeth, or loose crowns. Contact our direct 24/7 hotline at +91 9307512816 for immediate care.

Are your treatments painful? +−

Patient comfort is our top priority. With computer-assisted single-tooth anesthesia, numbing gels, micro-invasive ultrasonic tools, and optional nitrous oxide relaxation, more than 92% of our patients report a completely painless experience.

What dental services do you provide? +−

We offer full-scope comprehensive dental care: preventive hygiene checkups, porcelain veneers, professional in-office whitening, monolithic zirconia dental implants, root canal therapy, and Invisalign clear aligners.

Do you accept insurance? +−

Yes, we work with all major dental PPO insurance plans. We file electronic insurance claims on your behalf to maximize your reimbursement and offer 0% APR financing options through CareCredit.
