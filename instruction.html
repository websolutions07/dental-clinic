Patient Care & Recovery Instructions

Clinical recovery guidelines curated by Dr. Olivia, Dr. Rishi, and Dr. Emman at Apurva's Dental.

✨

1. Porcelain Veneers & Cosmetic Bonding

  • Avoid excessively hard foods (ice, hard candy, nutshells) for the first 48 hours following adhesive polymer curing.
  • Mild gingival tenderness around margins is normal and resolves within 2–3 days with warm salt-water rinses.
  • Maintain twice-daily brushing with non-abrasive fluoride toothpaste to protect veneer margins.
🩺

2. Root Canal Therapy & Crowns

  • Refrain from chewing on the treated tooth until your permanent protective crown is fully seated.
  • Take prescribed anti-inflammatory medication as directed to minimize periapical ligament sensitivity.
  • If temporary crown dislodges, contact our office immediately for prompt reseating.
🔩

3. Dental Implants & Surgical Extraction Care

  • Maintain firm pressure on the sterile surgical gauze pack for 45 minutes; do not spit forcefully or rinse vigorously for 24 hours.
  • Strictly avoid suction: Do not drink through straws or smoke for 7 days to preserve the protective blood clot and prevent dry socket.
  • Apply an external cold compress (15 mins on, 15 mins off) against the outer cheek during the first 24 hours to reduce swelling.
  • Stick to cool, soft foods (Greek yogurt, protein shakes, lukewarm broths) and avoid chewing on the surgical quadrant.
📐

4. Invisalign® Clear Aligner Protocols

  • Wear your aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day, removing them exclusively for meals and oral hygiene.
  • Clean your aligner trays using lukewarm water and a soft-bristle toothbrush; never use hot boiling water which warps medical thermoplastic.
  • Use aligner chewies for 3–5 minutes whenever inserting a new tray stage to verify intimate tooth contact and active tracking.
  • Always keep your previous set of trays in their travel case as an emergency backup.
🚨

5. Emergency Symptoms — When to Call 24/7

Contact Apurva's Dental 24/7 emergency clinical line (+91 9307512816) immediately if you experience:

  • Rapidly increasing facial or submandibular swelling, or persistent throbbing pain unresponsive to prescribed medication.
  • Continuous active bleeding that saturates gauze for more than 4 hours.
  • A loosened dental implant fixture, dislodged temporary restoration, or traumatic impact to treated teeth.
  • Persistent high body temperature or difficulty swallowing/opening the jaw.
