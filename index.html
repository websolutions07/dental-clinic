How often should I visit the dentist? +−

We recommend a routine dental checkup every six months to maintain optimal oral health, perform professional plaque cleaning, and catch any minor issues before they develop into major procedures.

Do you offer emergency dental services? +−

Yes, we prioritize same-day appointments for acute dental emergencies including severe toothache, chipped or fractured teeth, dental trauma, and loose crowns. Call our direct hotline at +91 9307512816 immediately.

Are your treatments painful? +−

Patient comfort is at the core of our philosophy. With modern computerized numbing, gentle topical gels, micro-invasive instruments, and optional relaxation sedation, over 92% of our patients report feeling completely comfortable and anxiety-free.

What dental services do you provide? +−

We provide full-spectrum family dental care under one roof: preventative cleanings, porcelain veneers, professional LED whitening, ceramic dental implants, root canal therapy, and Invisalign clear aligners.

Do you accept insurance? +−

Yes, we accept major private health insurance and dental PPO plans. Our front desk coordinates direct electronic claims processing on your behalf and provides transparent, itemized treatment estimates upfront.
